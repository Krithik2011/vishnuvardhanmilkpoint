# Milk Manager

Build a simple, mobile-friendly dairy distribution management app for a small Indian milk distributor. The owner works manually and is not technical, so the interface must be extremely clear and low-friction. Include: dashboard with today's sales/deliveries, outstanding customer balances, stock received and low-stock items; Customers section with customer name, phone, address, running balance, delivery/payment history; Products section with company/brand (Amul, Vijaya Telangana, Heritage, etc.), product name, unit, buy price and sale price; Stock received section to record supplier/company, products, quantities, purchase cost and date; Delivery/Sales section to quickly assign products and quantities to customers, calculate amount due and reduce stock; Payments section to record full/partial customer payments and update balance; reports / ledger view filterable by customer and date. Seed the app with realistic example data and make it fully usable as a frontend demo with local persistence. Use Indian rupees and clear date formatting. Add optional English/Telugu-friendly labels where useful, but prioritize readable English. Make the prominent primary actions: Add Customer, Record Stock, New Delivery, Record Payment. Avoid complex accounting terminology. Add simple safeguards such as confirmation before deletion and highlight overdue/outstanding accounts.
